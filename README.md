# ⚡ Pocket Battle — A Quick Pokémon Game

A tiny, self-contained Pokémon-style battle game. No build step, no
dependencies — just one HTML file.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. That's it.

## How to play

1. **Pick a partner** — Flamepup (Fire), Aquafin (Water), or Leaflet (Grass).
2. **Battle wild Pokémon** by choosing a move each turn.
3. **Win** to level up, gain max HP, and face a stronger opponent.
4. The run ends when your Pokémon faints — try to rack up as many wins as you can!

## Mechanics

- **Type matchups** — Fire > Grass > Water > Fire (and Electric > Water).
  Super-effective hits do 2× damage; resisted hits do ½.
- **STAB** — using a move that matches your type adds a 1.5× bonus.
- **Run Away** — 50% chance to flee to a fresh encounter; fail and the
  enemy gets a free hit.

Have fun! 🦊🐢🦎
