# Yeti Bottle Warranty Claim — Resolved

**Status:** Closed — replacement bottle received

## Summary

The green Yeti Rambler bottle stopped insulating: it sweated (condensation on the
outside) with cold contents, and ice melted within about two hours. This is the
classic symptom of a failed vacuum seal, which Yeti covers as a manufacturing
defect under the 5-year Rambler drinkware warranty.

## Timeline

- Bottle purchased at the YETI retail store, Ala Moana (e-receipt
  #010718260019104002308, emailed July 18, 2026 by YETI@orders.yeti.com).
- Vacuum-seal failure noticed: exterior condensation with ice water, poor
  cold retention compared with an identical newer bottle.
- Warranty claim filed with YETI (online claim form / 1-833-225-9384).
- Claim approved; replacement bottle received October 2026.

## Reference

- Warranty terms: https://www.yeti.com/warranty-information.html
- Claim portal: https://yeti.my.site.com/WarrantyClaim/s/
- Rambler drinkware warranty: 5 years from purchase, proof of purchase
  required; retail stores can reissue receipts by email/phone/card lookup.
